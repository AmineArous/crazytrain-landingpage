# Changelog

### `Initial Release`
# **Version 1.0**
**October 2026**

Welcome to **Crazy Train**! One stormy night in 1886, the night train starts rolling on its own toward a broken bridge. Stop it in time.

#### Features

- 🚂 **Five Scenes** — The caboose, the game car, the dining car, the mail car and the locomotive
- ⚡ **A Runaway Train** — No one at the controls, a storm overhead and a broken bridge ahead
- 🧩 **Puzzles in Every Object** — Soot-covered windows, chess, a wine cellar, stamps, wires in the dark, steam valves
- 🎲 **A New Game Each Time** — The caboose number, the chess game, the password and the pipes change every game
- 💡 **One Hint at a Time** — Matched to where you are, never the answer
- 🔓 **First Scene Free** — Unlock the full game with a single purchase
