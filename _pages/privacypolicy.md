# Privacy Policy

At **Crazy Train**, your privacy is our top priority.

<br>

## 1.0 What Stays on Your iPhone

Your progress in each scene and your settings (music and sound) are stored only on your device.

<br>

## 2.0 Anonymous Usage Statistics

To understand how the game is played and improve it, Crazy Train uses **Google Analytics for Firebase**. It records:

- When a scene is started, when it is completed, and the step reached when a scene is left unfinished  
- When a hint is opened, and the step of the scene it was opened at  
- When the end of the game is reached  
- When the full game offer is viewed, and the purchase of the full game (product and price)  
- When music or sound is switched on or off  
- Standard app events, such as app opens and sessions  

These events are tied to a random identifier generated for this installation of the App, not to your name, email or Apple Account. Google derives an approximate country or region from a masked IP address. The App does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not show ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. Google's handling of this data is described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

<br>

## 3.0 What We Don't Do

- ❌ We don't collect your name, email or contacts  
- ❌ We don't use advertising or the advertising identifier  
- ❌ We don't track you across other apps or websites  
- ❌ We don't share or sell any data to third parties for advertising  
- ❌ We don't require an account  

<br>

## 4.0 In-App Purchase

The first scene is free. The full game is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment information. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

<br>

## 5.0 Permissions

Crazy Train does not request access to your location, microphone, camera, photos, contacts or calendar.

<br>

## 6.0 Children's Privacy

Crazy Train does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The anonymous usage statistics described above are not tied to anyone's identity.

<br>

## 7.0 Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
